# Code of conduct

This is a place for people who use Threadnauts to get help, ask for things, and share what
they've made. Keep it a place people want to come back to.

- **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- **Help newcomers.** Everybody started with an empty colony.
- **Keep it on topic.** Threadnauts, the harnesses it reads, and working with coding agents.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post anyone else's threads, code, or personal details, and don't ask
  people for theirs.
- **No harassment.** That covers insults, slurs, unwanted attention, threats, and personal attacks
  of any kind, anywhere in this repo.
- **No spam.** That includes promotion that has nothing to do with the discussion.

The maintainers may edit, hide, or remove anything that breaks these rules, lock a thread, or
block someone from the repo. To report a problem privately, write to
[hello@threadcrumbs.io](mailto:hello@threadcrumbs.io).
