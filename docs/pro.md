# Threadnauts Pro

Threadnauts is free, with 24 worlds and the whole colony. **Threadnauts Pro** opens the other 56
worlds, and every world released while you subscribe. Prices and checkout are at
[threadnauts.io/pro](https://threadnauts.io/pro).

## What you keep

Every world released while you paid stays yours for good. Worlds released after your
subscription ends stay locked, and open again if you come back.

## Entering your key

1. Buy Pro at [threadnauts.io/pro](https://threadnauts.io/pro). Your license key is in the Polar
   receipt, and starts with `THREADCRUMBS-`.
2. In Threadnauts, open settings (`S`) and paste the key under **Threadnauts Pro**.
3. Threadnauts adds this computer to your license and downloads the Pro worlds.

## Computers

One key works on three computers. A computer is added the first time you enter your key on it,
and counts once, whichever Threadcrumbs app you enter the key in. To make room for another,
remove one in the [customer portal](https://threadcrumbs.io/portal), where you also manage your
subscription.

## Going offline

Entering your key needs the internet once, and so do new Pro worlds when they download. After
that, the license is checked on your computer, with no network call. Threadnauts renews it about
once a week while it's open and online, and being offline never turns Pro off.

## What Pro sends

Pro adds worlds and nothing else. When you enter your key, Threadnauts sends the key and a random
ID for this computer to the Threadcrumbs license service. After that, it sends only the license
itself, to renew it and to download Pro worlds. It never sends your threads, code, or project
names.
