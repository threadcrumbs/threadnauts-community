# Threadnauts Pro

Threadnauts is free, with 24 worlds, ten ships, and the whole colony. **Threadnauts Pro** opens the
other 56 worlds and two more ships, Race Condition and Zero Day, plus every world released while
you subscribe. Prices and checkout are at
[threadnauts.io/pro](https://threadnauts.io/pro).

## What you keep

Everything released while you paid stays yours for good. Anything released after your
subscription ends stays locked, and opens again if you come back.

## Entering your key

1. Buy Pro at [threadnauts.io/pro](https://threadnauts.io/pro). Your license key is in the Polar
   receipt, and starts with `THREADCRUMBS-`.
2. In Threadnauts, open settings (`S`) and paste the key under **Threadnauts Pro**.
3. Threadnauts adds this computer to your license and downloads the Pro content.

## Computers

One key works on three computers. A computer is added the first time you enter your key on it,
and counts once, whichever Threadcrumbs app you enter the key in. To make room for another,
remove one in the [customer portal](https://threadcrumbs.io/portal), where you also manage your
subscription.

## Going offline

Entering your key needs the internet once, and so does new Pro content when it downloads. After
that, the license is checked on your computer, with no network call. Threadnauts renews it about
once a week while it's open and online, and right away when you come back from Polar or choose
Check your plan in Settings › Threadnauts Pro. Being offline never turns Pro off.

## What Pro sends

Pro reads nothing more about your work. When you enter your key, Threadnauts sends the key and a random
ID for this computer to the Threadcrumbs license service. After that, it sends only the license
itself, to renew it and to download Pro content. It never sends your threads, code, or project
names.
