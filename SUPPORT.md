# Getting help

- **Start with [troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).** It covers missing repos and threads,
  Open doing nothing, ports, speed, and Pro.
- **Ask a question** in [Discussions](https://github.com/threadcrumbs/threadnauts-community/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Report a bug** with the [bug form](https://github.com/threadcrumbs/threadnauts-community/issues/new?template=bug.yml).
- **License keys, billing, and your computers** are in the
  [customer portal](https://threadcrumbs.io/portal). For anything the portal can't do, write to
  [hello@threadcrumbs.io](mailto:hello@threadcrumbs.io). Please don't post license keys in public.
