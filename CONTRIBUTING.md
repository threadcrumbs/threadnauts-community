# Taking part

Threadnauts is closed source, so this repo holds no app code. What it does hold is everything
around the app: bug reports, requests, docs, and the colonies people share. Here's how to help
with each.

## Bugs

[Report one](https://github.com/threadcrumbs/threadnauts-community/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
with the form. Three things make a bug fast to fix: your Threadnauts version, the steps that make
it happen, and a screenshot or a recording (`P` and `R` in the app).

**Your threads can be private.** Before you paste a screenshot, a log, or the harness list,
remove anything you don't want public: thread titles, prompts, repo names, and folder paths. A
screenshot from `P` leaves out the panels, but each zone's name plate still shows its repo's name.

## Harnesses and features

Search the [open issues](https://github.com/threadcrumbs/threadnauts-community/issues) first. A 👍
on an existing request tells us how many people want it, so it counts as much as a new one. A
comment about how you'd use it helps even more.

## Docs

If something in [docs](docs/) is wrong or unclear, open an issue saying what and where. If it's a
small fix, such as a typo, a broken link, or a step that's out of date, a pull request is welcome
too.

What you contribute is shared under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE), like the rest of this repo.

## Conduct

Be kind, and assume the person on the other end means well. The details are in the
[code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
